Contribute

Share something UWM should be able to reuse.

You do not need to know GitHub, open-source terminology, or repository conventions. Start with the problem and what you learned.

Six questions

01

What problem were you trying to solve?

02

What was the current practice?

03

What did you change or test?

04

What should another person be able to reuse?

05

What evidence or lesson came from using it?

06

Who should own and maintain it?

During the ART award, the TRANSLATE Program Manager can curate submissions and work with campus units to establish ownership.
